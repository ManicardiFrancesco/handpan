A MOMENT TO LISTEN

Three partials. One beautiful note.

The modeled handpan uses resonating steel modes, body resonance and sympathetic coupling. Its three principal modes are calibrated to 1:2:3. The isolated Fundamental, Octave and Fifth buttons play pure tones for checking their individual frequencies. Play on tap auditions fields while the microphone is off.

Select the tone field you’re testing, enable your microphone, and strike it gently. Allow about a second for the first precise reading.

  • Fundamental · 1× is the note’s main pitch.
  • Octave · 2× should be exactly twice its frequency.
  • Compound fifth · 3× sits an octave and a perfect fifth above the fundamental, exactly three times its frequency. It is a pure ratio, slightly different from an equal-tempered fifth.

The stripes drift left when flat and right when sharp. Still stripes mean in tune; a dash means that partial wasn’t detected. Cents show deviation from the ideal reference. The interval error compares each overtone with your measured fundamental.

Use headphones for reference playback. “Try a demo” analyzes a generated, intentionally detuned signal through the same detector. It doesn’t measure your instrument.
