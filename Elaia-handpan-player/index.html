QUICK GUIDE

Meet your handpan.

Play. Tap a tone field or a note button. Use more than one finger for chords. Tap the bare shell for a finger slap. Chord buttons choose the root octave; the notes you hear appear below.

Listen. Top notes, Bottom notes, and All notes play from lowest to highest. Tap Stop or play a note yourself to take over.

Explore. Drag the shell to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Flip to the bottom to discover another side. Right or middle drag always orbits, including while editing.

Make it yours. Choose Customize → Layout. The + button opens Add notes. Keep tapping Add to continue up the scale; Done opens the last field for editing. Unassigned creates a silent field to tune later. Top notes fill default positions, then form a symmetric inner cluster of up to five. Dragging, resizing, rotating, or changing a field's shell gives you manual control. Original restores the factory instrument; Undo brings your edits back. Open Sound & tuning for 432/440 Hz and transpose.

Keep it. Your current layout stays on this device automatically. Save & get link keeps a named copy and gives you a URL that recreates it, including every custom field.

Keyboard. Space plays the first field, then A through ;, then Q through P. Hold Shift for a soft touch; Alt for a stronger strike. M damps. Esc exits editing.
